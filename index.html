FAQs

What is the cost of a consultation?

Our initial consultation is completely free of charge. A member of our team will visit the site to assess the job and discuss your needs—no obligation, no cost.

What role do clients play in the design process?

Your input is a key part of the process. We’ll work with you from the start—going over styles, colors, and finishes—and check in along the way to make sure everything matches your vision and preferences.

How long does the design process typically take?

Timelines can vary depending on the size and complexity of the job. That said, I always aim to work efficiently while maintaining a high level of detail and quality throughout the project.

How are project budgets managed?

We’ll discuss your budget up front and provide a clear estimate before any work begins. I track expenses throughout the project and keep you informed if anything changes, so there are no surprises.
